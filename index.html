FAQ

Common Questions

AK-47 (Avtomat Kalashnikova 1947) Soviet Union mein Mikhail Kalashnikov ne design ki thi. Yeh 7.62x39mm intermediate cartridge use karti hai. Duniya ki sabse zyada produced aur widely used assault rifle hai — estimated 100 million+ units banaye gaye hain. Iski reliability, simplicity aur low production cost ne isko global icon banaya.

Glock duniya ki sabse popular pistol series hai. 65+ countries ki military aur police forces isko use karti hain. Glock 17 sabse mashhoor model hai. Iski popularity ki wajah polymer frame (lightweight), safe-action trigger system (3 safety mechanisms), aur minimal parts count (33 vs 50+ in traditional pistols) hai.

Modern sniper rifles ki effective range bahut varied hai. Barrett M82 ki effective range 1,800 meters tak hai. CheyTac M200 2,000+ meters pe accurate hai. Longest confirmed sniper kill 3,540 meters tak record hai (Canadian sniper, 2017). .50 BMG aur specialized calibers extreme ranges achieve karte hain.

Assault rifle intermediate cartridge use karti hai (7.62x39mm AK-47, 5.56x45mm M4) — controlled recoil aur automatic fire ke liye designed. Battle rifle full-power cartridge use karti hai (7.62x51mm NATO FN FAL, G3) — zyada range aur stopping power but zyada recoil. Modern trend assault rifles ki taraf shift hua hai.

Desert Eagle sabse powerful production handgun hai jo .50 Action Express fire karti hai. Gas-operated system use karta hai (jo normally rifles mein hota hai), isliye .50 BMG level energy deliver kar sakti hai. Movies aur video games (CS:GO, GTA) mein zyada dikhne ki wajah se pop culture icon ban gayi hai.

Shotguns primarily close-quarters battle (CQB) mein use hote hain. Military use: door breaching, room clearing, trench warfare. Law enforcement: riot control (less-lethal rounds), hostage situations. Civilian use: hunting, home defense, sport shooting (trap/skeet). 12 gauge sabse common caliber hai.
